# Meanings and example attribution

Dictionary records from **Wiktionary contributors** and retained dictionary-usage sentences are available under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International**.

- [License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- [Wiktionary copyright policy](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Copyrights)

Each dictionary record preserves its page title, revision ID, source URL and credit. Additional base-word source revisions are retained when a form explanation uses them. Each retained dictionary example has its own source URL and license metadata. Changes include converting wiki formatting to plain text, removing unsupported template fragments, selecting at most two senses, expanding forms from linked base entries, and masking target spellings in recall clues. Dictionary content and its adaptations retain CC BY-SA 4.0.

Records marked `sourceType: study-note` are short original explanations written for Molly, checked against factual study context and, when needed, linked dictionaries or subject references. They are labelled **Original study wording** and distinguished in the interface. They are not copied Hoover-slide paragraphs or replacements claimed to be official competition definitions.

Examples marked `type: original` are newly written contextual practice sentences, labelled **Original example wording**. They were independently checked for the target spelling, grammar, intended sense and suitability for Molly.

The original Hoover slide prose, example sentences, illustrations and recording files are not bundled. Every word links to its study slide; recording URLs request the source-hosted public audio only when asked. The linked collection mixes source years and is not a verified official 2027 list. Review does not certify every rare-word pronunciation or the precise sense a competition pronouncer will choose.
